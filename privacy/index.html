Privacy Notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This notice explains how I handle personal information when you visit this website or contact me through it. I have written it to be clear and readable. If anything is unclear, please email me and I will be glad to explain.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Danielle Leite Richardson, Professional Counselor registered with AssoCounseling (member code A3618), based in Florence, Italy.

Email: danivegaleite@gmail.com

I have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as this is not required for my work.

2. What information I collect

When you use the contact form

  • Your name and email address.
  • Optionally, where you are based and your preferred language.
  • Your message. Because of the nature of counselling, you may choose to mention your health, wellbeing, relationships or personal circumstances. Under data protection law, information about health is a special category of data that needs extra protection.

Please share only what you are comfortable with. A few words about what you are looking for is enough. There is no need to go into detail before we speak.

When you visit the website

  • Technical data: like any website, my hosting provider automatically records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to deliver the site securely.
  • Analytics: only if you accept analytics cookies, I use Google Analytics to understand, in general terms, how visitors use the site, for example which pages are read most. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browsing activity on this site. I do not use it to identify you personally.

3. Why I use your information, and the legal basis

  • Replying to your enquiry and arranging a free introductory call: this is a step you have asked me to take before any counselling agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For any health or other special category information you choose to include, the legal basis is your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the box on the contact form (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  • Running the website securely: my legitimate interest in keeping the site working and protected from misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Website analytics: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code).

Giving your details is voluntary. Without them, I simply cannot reply to your enquiry.

If you go on to become a client, you will receive my full informed consent and privacy notice for counselling, which covers sessions, session notes and billing.

4. Who can see your information

Your information is never sold or published, and is never used for marketing. The content of your message is read only by me. It is processed on my behalf by these service providers, acting as data processors:

  • Netlify, Inc. hosts this website, receives contact form submissions and stores them securely in my account.
  • Google LLC provides my email, through which form notifications reach me, and Google Analytics. The website's fonts are also loaded from Google's servers, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google.

Information may also be disclosed where the law requires it, for example to a judicial or supervisory authority.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Netlify and Google may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), including its UK Extension, and on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (Art. 46 GDPR). You can ask me for more information about these safeguards.

6. How long I keep your information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to counselling: deleted from the form system and from my email within 6 months of your last contact.
  • If you become a client: your enquiry becomes part of your client record, kept as set out in my client privacy notice.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google for the period set in my Google Analytics account, and no longer than 14 months.

7. How your information is protected

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to form submissions and email is protected by password and limited to me. I follow the confidentiality standards of the AssoCounseling Code of Ethics.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, and the UK GDPR if you live in the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • ask whether I hold information about you, and receive a copy of it (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate information corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your information deleted (Art. 17);
  • ask me to limit how I use your information (Art. 18);
  • receive your information in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to how I use your information (Art. 21);
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything done lawfully before you withdrew it (Art. 7(3)).

To use any of these rights, email danivegaleite@gmail.com. It is free, and I will reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. If you live in the UK, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office, and elsewhere in the EU, the authority in your country.

9. Cookies

This website itself does not set cookies to identify you. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Google Analytics only runs, and its cookies are only set, if you click Accept. Your choice is saved in your browser so you are not asked on every page.

You can change your choice at any time: cookie settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt out add on.

10. Automated decisions

No automated decision making or profiling is carried out.

11. Changes to this notice

I may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
